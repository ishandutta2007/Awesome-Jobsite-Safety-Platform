# Awesome-Jobsite-Safety-Platform

## Top Jobsite Safety Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Construction Safety, PPE Compliance, Inductions, Observations, Incident Reporting, AI Site Monitoring & OSHA-Ready Workflows*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Jobsite Safety**. These systems help general contractors and trades manage worker orientations, pre-task plans, inspections, observations, incident reporting, subcontractor compliance, and increasingly AI-powered site monitoring.



**Examples** include HammerTech, SafeSite, Smartvid.io, Intenseye, Procore Safety, SafetyCulture, Newmetrix, Eyrus, WakeCap, and SiteAware (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full construction safety platforms are predominantly commercial. Practical open options include **hazard-management systems** (SafetIbase), **AI PPE detection** prototypes, **OSHA data analytics** tools, and general incident frameworks. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[HammerTech](https://www.hammertech.com/)**  

  Construction-native safety and site-operations platform covering inductions, pre-task plans, observations, incidents, and subcontractor workflows.



- **[SafeSite](https://www.safesitehq.com/)**  

  Mobile-first safety management platform used for inspections, observations, and incident tracking on construction and industrial sites.



- **[Smartvid.io](https://www.smartvid.io/)**  

  AI-powered construction safety and risk platform that analyzes site imagery and data for hazard and compliance insights.



- **[Intenseye](https://www.intenseye.com/)**  

  Computer-vision workplace safety platform that monitors PPE compliance and unsafe conditions in real time.



- **[Procore Safety](https://www.procore.com/)**  

  Safety management capabilities inside the Procore construction management suite—convenient when teams already live in Procore.



- **[SafetyCulture (iAuditor)](https://safetyculture.com/)**  

  Inspection and checklist platform widely used across industries, including construction, for audits, observations, and issue tracking.



- **[Newmetrix](https://www.newmetrix.com/)**  

  AI visual intelligence platform for construction risk and safety insights derived from site photos and video.



- **[Eyrus](https://www.eyrus.com/)**  

  Jobsite intelligence platform providing workforce and activity visibility that supports safety and productivity programs.



- **[WakeCap](https://www.wakecap.com/)**  

  IoT and wearable-oriented platform for workforce tracking and safety on construction sites.



- **[SiteAware](https://www.siteaware.com/)**  

  Construction verification and quality/safety platform using reality capture and digital twin approaches.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[SafetIbase](https://github.com/safetibase/safetibase)**  

  Award-winning open-source system (AGPL) for identifying, managing, and communicating health and safety hazards on construction projects (SharePoint-oriented).



- **[Construction Safety AI (PPE detection)](https://github.com/808cadger/construction-safety-ai)**  

  Open computer-vision solution for PPE detection, automated alerts, and violation management on construction sites.



- **[CSAT – Construction Safety Analytics Tool](https://github.com/jim-kinter/csat-construction-safety-analytics-tool)**  

  Open analytics platform that turns public OSHA incident data into descriptive and predictive safety insights.



- **[Incident Bot and open incident frameworks](https://github.com/incidentbot/incidentbot)**  

  Open-source incident management frameworks (ChatOps-oriented) that teams can adapt for safety incident coordination.



- **[Checklist and inspection open apps](https://github.com/)**  

  Community mobile and web checklist tools usable for toolbox talks, inspections, and observation logging.



- **[OSHA and regulatory data open parsers](https://github.com/)**  

  Libraries and pipelines for ingesting and analyzing public OSHA or equivalent regulatory datasets.



- **[Hazard register open templates](https://github.com/)**  

  Open templates and simple databases for maintaining project hazard registers and residual-risk tracking.



- **[Toolbox-talk and training open content tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Tools for managing safety meeting content, attendance, and acknowledgements.



- **[Wearable and IoT safety open prototypes](https://github.com/)**  

  Experimental open projects connecting sensors or wearables to safety alerts (require careful validation).



- **[Photo-based observation open collectors](https://github.com/)**  

  Lightweight apps for capturing geo-tagged safety observations and photos from the field.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Deploying **SafetIbase** for structured hazard identification and communication on projects that can use SharePoint.

- Experimenting with **open PPE computer-vision** models for research or pilot camera-based monitoring.

- Using **CSAT-style analytics** on public incident data to inform proactive safety programs.

- Combining open checklists with commercial platforms for field data capture.

- Accepting that full induction workflows, subcontractor prequalification, OSHA log generation, multi-site governance, and production AI monitoring still favor commercial platforms (HammerTech, Procore Safety, SafetyCulture, Intenseye, Smartvid.io, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on transparent hazard management and analytics rather than replacing enterprise safety suites.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Capture observations and incidents via open or commercial mobile tools → maintain a hazard register (SafetIbase or similar) → analyze trends with open OSHA/analytics tools → escalate serious incidents in a managed platform. Suitable for safety teams with development support. Most general contractors continue to adopt commercial jobsite safety platforms for scale and compliance readiness.
